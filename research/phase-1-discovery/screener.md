# Recruitment Screener

**Purpose:** filter ~20 interested respondents down to 6 who match our target persona and diversity goals.
**Delivery:** Google Form or Typeform; linked from all recruitment posts.
**Time to complete:** under 2 minutes.

---

**Intro copy:**

> Hi! We're researching how recreational golfers use their phones at the driving range. If you're picked, it's a 30-minute call (Zoom or phone) and we'll send you a $30 gift card for your time. No sales pitch, no follow-up spam. Five quick questions:

---

## Questions

**1. How often do you play golf (range or course)?**
- [ ] Multiple times a week
- [ ] About once a week
- [ ] 1–3 times a month
- [ ] Less than once a month *(screen out)*
- [ ] Never *(screen out)*

**2. Have you been to a driving range in the last 60 days?**
- [ ] Yes
- [ ] No *(screen out)*

**3. How would you describe your skill level?**
- [ ] Beginner (still learning the basics)
- [ ] Intermediate (consistent contact, working on specifics)
- [ ] Advanced (low handicap / competitive)

**4. When you go to the range, is it usually...**
- [ ] Alone
- [ ] With friends/family
- [ ] A mix of both

**5. What's your primary phone?**
- [ ] Android — which model? [text]
- [ ] iPhone — which model? [text]

**6. Have you ever used a golf-swing analysis app?**
- [ ] Yes — which one(s)? [text]
- [ ] No
- [ ] I've tried filming myself but never used a dedicated app

**7. Contact info**
- Name: [text]
- Email or phone: [text]
- Best times to chat (loose is fine): [text]

---

## Internal scoring (not shown to respondent)

Weight responses to hit the diversity matrix in `README.md`. When in doubt, favor:
- Mix of skill levels (not all beginners)
- Mix of solo/friends preferences
- Mix of OS
- Non-obvious: at least 1 person who's tried an analysis app and at least 1 who hasn't
