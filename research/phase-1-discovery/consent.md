# Participant Consent (Phase 1 Discovery Interview)

**Delivery:** verbal + written confirmation at the start of the call. Written copy sent as a link before the session.

---

## Plain-English consent script (read aloud at start of interview)

> Thanks for making time today. Before we get going, quick housekeeping so you know what's happening:
>
> 1. This is a research chat. We're exploring a possible product — there's nothing to buy and nothing for you to commit to.
> 2. I'd like to record the audio so we don't have to take notes in real time. The recording stays inside our team and gets deleted within 90 days. We don't publish it, share it, or use it to train anything.
> 3. Any quotes we use in our internal notes will be anonymized — first name only or a pseudonym, your choice.
> 4. You can skip any question. You can stop at any time. You'll get the $30 either way.
> 5. If you say something you'd rather we forget, just say "off the record" and we'll strike it.
>
> Is all of that okay with you? Can I start the recording?
