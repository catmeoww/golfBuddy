## Through Annie's husband (the coach) — word of mouth

> Ask his regular students if they'd be open to a 30-min research chat. Frame as "a friend is building something, wants to hear how golfers actually practice." Offer the gift card. Cap at 2 participants from this channel to avoid a biased sample.

## Sources NOT to use

- Paid recruitment panels like UserTesting — too removed from the target, sample tends to be career testers rather than actual golfers
- Golf coaches and pros — wrong persona (they'd be v1.1 coach-mode research, separate study)
- Driving ranges the team has a commercial relationship with — selection bias
