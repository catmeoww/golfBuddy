# Phase 1 — Discovery Research Kit

**Goal:** validate the problem and pressure-test MVP scope before writing code.
**Owner:** Jordan (PM) + Lin (UX)
**Target:** 6 interviews, 30 min each, completed in 1–2 weeks.

## Files

- [`screener.md`](screener.md) — 6-question recruitment screener
- [`recruitment.md`](recruitment.md) — posts for Reddit, range bulletins, network asks
- [`consent.md`](consent.md) — participant consent (short form)
- [`interview-script.md`](interview-script.md) — the 30-minute interview protocol
- [`synthesis-template.md`](synthesis-template.md) — one per participant, fills up during the interview
- [`insights-report-template.md`](insights-report-template.md) — final deliverable after all 6 interviews

## Process

1. **Recruit** — post in 3 channels, screen replies, aim for 6 participants that satisfy the diversity matrix below
2. **Schedule** — 30 min blocks, Zoom or phone, record with consent
3. **Interview** — one person facilitates (Jordan), one notes (Lin). Swap for variety.
4. **Transcribe** — auto-transcribe, light cleanup. Fill `synthesis-template.md` per participant within 24h.
5. **Synthesize** — after all 6, cluster insights into themes, write `insights-report-template.md`
6. **Decide** — team reviews. PRD gets revised. Open questions in `00-team-kickoff.md` get answered or re-parked.

## Diversity matrix (recruit against this)

| Dimension | Target mix (of 6) |
|---|---|
| Solo vs. with-friends preference | ≥ 2 each |
| Android vs. iOS | ≥ 2 each |
| Age | at least 2 in 25–35, 2 in 36–50 |
| Skill level (self-reported) | beginner / intermediate / advanced — ≥ 1 each |
| Tried a swing-analysis app before | ≥ 2 yes, ≥ 2 no |
| Gender | mixed; don't recruit all-male sample |

## Timeline

- Day 1–3: post screeners, collect ~20 responses
- Day 4: schedule 6
- Day 5–10: conduct interviews
- Day 11–12: synthesis
- Day 13: team review + PRD update
