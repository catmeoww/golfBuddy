# Recruitment Screener — Coach persona (primary)

**Purpose:** filter ~20 interested respondents down to 6 hobby / parent coaches who match our validated primary persona (M) and hit our diversity goals.
**Delivery:** Google Form or Typeform; linked from all recruitment posts.
**Time to complete:** under 2 minutes.

**Context:** As of 2026-04-19, after P01 discovery synthesis, our primary persona is a hobby coach (parent, informal, not-for-income). We retain `screener.md` (recreational-golfer persona) as a secondary / alt screener if we later need to widen the ICP.

---

**Intro copy:**

> Hi! We're researching how people who coach golf — even informally — record, organize, and review swings. If you're picked, it's a 30-minute call (Zoom or phone) and we'll send you a $30 gift card for your time. No sales pitch, no follow-up spam. A few quick questions:

## Internal scoring (not shown to respondent)

Target mix across the 6 recruits:

- **Student type:** at least 3 who coach a child (matches primary persona); at least 1 who coaches an adult peer (tests generalization).
- **Venue:** at least 3 "backyard / home" to match the validated context; at least 1 range or indoor sim for robustness.
- **Recording behavior:** at least 2 who record regularly (they'll have the most opinions on tooling), at least 2 who almost never record (they'll tell us about friction that kills the behavior).
- **Tournament context:** at least 2 whose student competes (validates the tournament feature); at least 1 whose student does not (tests whether the feature feels mandatory or optional).
- **OS:** at least 2 Android, 2 iOS.

When in doubt, favor the profile closest to M: parent, coaches a child in a backyard, child competes, Android. M himself is already our P01 — don't re-recruit; recruit five more distinct profiles.

## Calibration note

If we can't find enough parent-coaches of competitive young golfers (which is the narrow-ICP risk called out in the PRD), widen to "anyone who coaches another person on their golf swing at least monthly" before falling back to the recreational-golfer screener.
