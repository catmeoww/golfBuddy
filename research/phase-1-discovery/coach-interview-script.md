# Discovery Interview Script — Coach Persona

**Duration:** 30 minutes
**Goal:** understand how the coach actually coaches today, what tools fit into his workflow, and where the friction is — WITHOUT pitching our product.
**Participant type:** part-time or full-time golf coach. Anyone who regularly gives swing feedback to another person.

## Same guiding principles as the golfer script

No leading questions. No demos. Ask about past behavior, not future wants. Follow up with "why" 2–3 times.

---

## 0. Warm-up (3 min)

> "Thanks for making time. Before we dive in, tell me a bit about yourself — where you live, what you do, and how coaching fits into that."

*Purpose:* frame coaching as one piece of their life, understand relative weight (full-time vs. side, family vs. outside students).

---

## 1. Their coaching practice (5 min)

> "Walk me through who you coach these days."

*Probes:*
- How many students?
- Who are they — kids, adults, family, strangers?
- How often do you see each one?
- How long have you been coaching them?
- Do you charge? Informal vs. formal?

> "How do people usually find you, or how did you find them?"

> "Where do you actually coach — range, course, home, other?"

---

## 2. A real coaching session — detailed walkthrough (10 min)

**The critical question.** Specifics, not generalities.

> "Tell me about the most recent coaching session you did. In as much detail as you can remember — who, when, where, what you did first, what you did next."

*Probes (ask only if not covered naturally):*
- What did you work on with them?
- How did you decide that was the thing to work on?
- Did you use your phone? Camera? Any tech?
- Did you record anything?
  - If yes: what did you do with it — show them in the moment, send later, keep for yourself?
  - If no: why not this session? Ever? What would have to change?
- Did you give them anything to take away? Verbal cues, written notes, video?
- How long did the session last?
- Did you do any prep before? Any follow-up after?

**Golden follow-ups:**
- "You mentioned [X] — walk me through that more."
- "Why did you [action] rather than [alternative]?"
- "What was the student's reaction to that?"

---

## 3. Tools, workflow, pain points (7 min)

> "What tools or tech do you use to coach? Anything — phone, apps, trackers, paper, physical equipment."

*Probes:*
- Trackers / launch monitors — which, how often, how useful?
- Apps — which, for what, what's annoying about them?
- Video — how do you record, where does it live, who sees it?
- Do you share anything with students between sessions? How?

> "What's the most frustrating part of your coaching work?"

> "Is there anything about coaching you'd like to do better but currently can't?"

*Probes:*
- Seeing a problem you can't articulate?
- Communicating something a student doesn't "feel" yet?
- Tracking improvement across weeks?
- Dealing with students between sessions?

> "If you had to teach a student remotely — how would you even do it today?"

---

## 4. Ethan specifically (if the coach is the father) (3 min)

> "Coaching your own kid — how's that different from coaching anyone else?"

*Probes:*
- Does the dynamic change anything about what tools you want?
- Tournaments — what do you do the day of, the day before, the day after?
- When Ethan plateaus or struggles, how do you diagnose it?

---

## 5. Wishlist — open-ended (2 min)

> "If you could wave a wand and change one thing about how you coach, what would it be?"

> "Anything you wish your students would do that they don't?"

*Don't steer toward apps or video. Let him take it where it goes.*

---

## 6. Close

> "Anything I didn't ask about that I should have?"

> "If I have one follow-up question in a week, is it okay to message you?"

---

## What we specifically need to learn from this interview

> Hidden notes for the facilitator — things the team needs answered to replan the product.

1. **Is video review part of his workflow today?** If yes — which tool? If no — does he want it, or has he tried and abandoned it? (A "tried and abandoned" answer is the most important signal.)
2. **Is the student 1-to-1 Ethan, or is there a 1-to-many coach workflow emerging?** Determines whether we need multi-student features (rosters, sharing, annotations) or a simpler father/son tool.
3. **Where's the biggest unmet need?** Capture, review, annotation, sharing, progress tracking, or something else we haven't considered?
4. **Does he already have a solution he's happy with?** The backyard tracker — does it cover video too? Does it cover what video would? If so, we might be solving a non-problem.
5. **Would he actually use a new app?** Coaches often have workflows they won't change. Signal from the interview: does he already adopt new tools (yes → promising) or does he stick to what he knows (no → we're likely building something he won't use)?

## Red flags to watch for

- If every answer is "video review is great, I'd love that" — he's being polite. Push harder on specifics.
- If he never mentions anything digital unprompted — he may not want a digital tool at all.
- If the biggest pain is something we can't solve (e.g., student motivation, weather) — don't pretend our product addresses it.
